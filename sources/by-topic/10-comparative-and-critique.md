# Sources — 10 Comparative and critique

Bibliography for `kb/10-comparative-and-critique/`. Compiled 2026-09-20. Grouped by topic; every
entry is cited from at least one note in the section.

Entries marked `[unverified]` were identified from a search result, a publisher listing, an abstract
page or another paper's reference list, but the full text was not retrieved during this research
run; bibliographic fields are reported as found. Entries marked "full text read" were downloaded and
read as extracted text during this run, and are the ones the section's verbatim quotations come from.

## Higher-order networks, hypergraph mathematics, representation choice

- Aksoy, S. G., Joslyn, C., Ortiz Marrero, C., Praggastis, B., Purvine, E. "Hypernetwork science via high-order hypergraph walks." *EPJ Data Science* 9(1):16, 2020. arXiv:1906.11295. https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.11295 — **full text read.** Source of the hypergraph↔bicolored-graph bijection, the 2^k bicolorings point, hypergraph duality, and "hypergraph-to-graph reductions are inevitably lossy".
- Battiston, F., Cencetti, G., Iacopini, I., Latora, V., Lucas, M., Patania, A., Young, J.-G., Petri, G. "Networks beyond pairwise interactions: structure and dynamics." *Physics Reports* 874:1–92, 2020. arXiv:2006.01764. https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.01764 — **full text read.** Source of the bipartite-representation passage, the incidence-matrix/bipartite-adjacency identity, the clustering-coefficient point, and the hypergraph definition.
- Benson, A. R., Gleich, D. F., Higham, D. J. "Higher-order Network Analysis Takes Off, Fueled by Classical Ideas and New Data." arXiv:2103.05031, 8 March 2021 (based on an article published online by *SIAM News*). https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.05031 — **full text read.** Source of the "connected components reduce, width-2 walks do not" argument and the three-question disease-propagation example.
- Chodrow, P. S. "Configuration Models of Random Hypergraphs." arXiv:1902.09302, submitted 25 Feb 2019, revised 13 Dec 2019. https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.09302 — abstract read verbatim. Source of "randomizing over hypergraph space rather than projected graph space … can dramatically alter statistical inference and study findings".
- Torres, L., Blevins, A. S., Bassett, D. S., Eliassi-Rad, T. "The why, how, and when of representations for complex systems." *SIAM Review* 63(3):435–485, 2021. DOI 10.1137/20M1355896. Preprint (dated 12 Feb 2021): http://www.eliassi.org/papers/torres-blevins-sirev-2021.pdf — **full text read.** Source of the "forgetting independent relations" analysis, the lossy/assumption-adding translation ladder, and the warning against choosing a formalism because tools exist.
- Hajij, M., Zamzmi, G., Papamarkou, T., Miolane, N., Guzmán-Sáenz, A., Ramamurthy, K. N., Birdal, T., Dey, T. K., Mukherjee, S., Samaga, S. N., Livesay, N., Walters, R., Rosen, P., Schaub, M. T. "Topological Deep Learning: Going Beyond Graph Data." arXiv:2206.00606, 1 June 2022 (rev. 19 May 2023). https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.00606 — abstract read. Combinatorial complexes "generalize and combine useful traits of both hypergraphs and cell complexes".
- Johnson, J. "Hypernetworks of Complex Systems." In *Complex Sciences*, Springer LNICST, 2009. https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-642-02466-5_35 — `[unverified]` publisher page returned an authentication redirect; only search-result metadata and secondary descriptions were consulted. Book: *Hypernetworks in the Science of Complex Systems*, Imperial College Press (2009/2013 editions).
- Gil Pons, R., Ward, M., Miller, L. "Finding (s,d)-Hypernetworks in F-Hypergraphs is NP-Hard." arXiv:2201.04799, 2022. https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.04799 — `[unverified]` record taken from `sources/by-topic/01-foundations.md`; cited here only for the competing sense of "hypernetwork".
- Joslyn, C., Nowak, K. "Ubergraphs: A Definition of a Recursive Hypergraph Structure." arXiv:1704.05547, submitted 18 April 2017 (PNNL-26402). https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.05547 — abstract read verbatim. Note: frequently mis-cited as "Joslyn and Aksoy".
- Kivelä, M., Arenas, A., Barthelemy, M., Gleeson, J. P., Moreno, Y., Porter, M. A. "Multilayer Networks." *Journal of Complex Networks* 2(3):203–271, 2014. arXiv:1309.7233. https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.7233 — abstract read.
- Praggastis, B., Aksoy, S., Arendt, D., Bonicillo, M., Joslyn, C., Purvine, E., Shapiro, M., Yun, J. Y. "HyperNetX: A Python package for modeling complex network data as hypergraphs." arXiv:2310.11626, 17 October 2023. https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11626 — abstract read.
- Coll, M., Joslyn, C. A., Landry, N. W., Lotito, Q. F., Myers, A., Pickard, J., Praggastis, B., Szufel, P. "HIF: The hypergraph interchange format for higher-order networks." *Network Science* 13:e21, 2025. arXiv:2507.11520. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11520 — `[unverified]` full citation taken from `sources/by-topic/01-foundations.md`; the repository below was read directly.

## Knowledge representation: n-ary facts, reification, hyper-relational models

- Boley, H. "Directed recursive labelnode hypergraphs: A new representation-language." *Artificial Intelligence* 9(1):49–85, 1977. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/0004370277900145 — `[unverified]` abstract only; record taken from `kb/02-knowledge-representation/what-is-a-knowledge-hypergraph.md`.
- Fatemi, B., Taslakian, P., Vazquez, D., Poole, D. "Knowledge Hypergraphs: Prediction Beyond Binary Relations." IJCAI 2020. arXiv:1906.00137 (v3, 15 July 2020). https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.00137 — **full text read.** Source of the JF17K arity breakdown (Table 4), the r-SimplE collapse at arity ≥ 3, the star-to-clique counterexample, and the statement that reification "does not lose information during conversion".

EMNLP 2020. arXiv:2009.10847. https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.10847 — **full text read.** Source of the hyper-relational/hypergraph distinction, the combinatorial-explosion argument, the JF17K 44.5% leakage measurement, and the WD50K construction.
- Hayes, J., Gutierrez, C. "Bipartite Graphs as Intermediate Model for RDF." ISWC 2004. https://users.dcc.uchile.cl/~cgutierr/papers/bipartite.pdf — `[unverified]` cited via `kb/02-knowledge-representation/what-is-a-knowledge-hypergraph.md`.
- Hu, Z., Gutiérrez-Basulto, V., Xiang, Z., Li, R., Pan, J. Z. "HyperMono: A Monotonicity-aware Approach to Hyper-Relational Knowledge Representation." arXiv:2404.09848, 15 April 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.09848 — abstract read; source of the qualifier-monotonicity statement.
- Iordanov, B. "HyperGraphDB: A Generalized Graph Database." *WAIM 2010 Workshops*, LNCS 6185, Springer, 2010. https://hypergraphdb.org/docs/hypergraphdb.pdf — `[unverified]` cited via `kb/02-knowledge-representation/what-is-a-knowledge-hypergraph.md`.

## Method note

Claims in this section of the form "X does not cite Y" or "the word Z does not appear" were produced
by case-insensitive string search over text extracted from the published PDFs on 2026-09-20. PDF text
extraction can drop ligatures and split hyphenated words across lines, so a count of zero means "no
match in the extracted text", not a proof of absence. The documents searched were the arXiv PDFs of
Battiston et al. 2020, Torres et al. 2021, Aksoy et al. 2020, Fatemi et al. 2020, Galkin et al. 2020,
Wei et al. 2025, Luo et al. 2025, Feng et al. 2025 and Xiang et al. 2026.
