# P6 re-run — the observed schemas on P3a's exact counts

Project folder: [projects/p6-schema-width/](../../projects/p6-schema-width/). Previous log:
[2026-09-25-p6-gate.md](2026-09-25-p6-gate.md), whose "What remains" named this re-run. Times are UTC.

## Brief

P6's observed Wikidata schemas were built from SQID's qualifier counts (dump of 2026-08-10) over
DeltaBot's main-statement counts (2026-09-23): two dates, and a third-party count whose unit was
unverified. P3a has now counted qualifier usage exactly, in one pass over the Wikidata JSON dump of
2026-09-22, under the shared naming wd-roles r1. The re-run replaces the SQID-based observed rows
with P3a-based ones, adds the eight rows on P3a's corpus slice (the items with an English Wikipedia
article), and carries the numbers into the publication draft and the base. The procedure is
[RELEASE.md](../../projects/p6-schema-width/publication/RELEASE.md) §1.

## What ran

1. **Input (2026-09-25, `13e5bdb`).**
   [qualifier-usage-20260922.json](../../projects/p3a-clean-nary-corpus/qualifier-usage-20260922.json),
   format `p3a-qualifier-usage/1`, sha256 `c76f8b38…b634b`. Every figure the P3a session reported
   was checked in the file before use:
   - `all`: 13,317 relations, 1,778,475,846 main statements, 78,971 (relation, qualifier) entries;
   - `kept`: 12,707 relations, 138,848,175 statements (the 7,584,990 kept items are P3a's README
     figure);
   - P39: 1,993,604 statements; P580 on 1,292,907, P582 on 986,437, P2937 on 690,298, P1365 on 331,437;
   - rule 6a: relation P580 carries P582 on 48 statements and itself on 7;
   - over the dump: 14,869 statements left out under 6b, 98,453 mismatched rank reasons (6c), 39,687
     self-qualified statements.

   One detail differs from the report: the 57 rule-8 properties are in `out_of_scope`, and only the
   45 of them with main statements are in `out_of_scope_relations`.
2. **Environment.**
   - A fresh venv from `git archive HEAD`, so that another session's edits to `src/khg_contracts/`
     could not reach the run.
   - `build-solvers.sh` built BalancedGo `872c662` and log-k-decomp `5e021dd` from GitHub. Both
     binaries are byte-identical to the first run's.
3. **The run (03:33 to 05:56, 2 h 23 min).** `reproduce.sh --p3a-counts … --rows observed --jobs 2`,
   with the same limits as before: 600 s per step, 120 s per solver attempt, 1,200 s of solver time
   per row, bisection on k.
   - Two rows at a time, not three: another session's database servers shared the four CPUs.
   - Each report and `results/machine.json` record the load. Other processes used 0.5 cores on
     average (median 0.2, at most 2.2); the survey itself used 1.8.
4. **Install.**
   - The declared and Biolink schema files regenerated byte-identical and were left alone.
   - The four dump-scope observed files were rebuilt from P3a's counts. The SQID-based files and
     their rows' reports moved unchanged to `results/superseded/` (24 files).
   - The four slice files are new.
   - All 16 observed rows ran with status 0.
5. **Checks.**
   - khg-width's tests: 108 pass, and the survey tests re-validate every certificate and lower-bound
     witness of all 23 rows.
   - `results/pending-rerun.json` is gone, and no row has `counts_source` = `SQID`.
6. **Commits.** Results: `c065478`. Documents and base: the commit after it.

## What changed against the SQID-based rows

- **Relations: 13,608 → 13,315.**
  - DeltaBot counts main statements on every entity type. P3a's `all` scope is the items and
    property entities of the dump.
  - So 294 lexicographic properties drop out: dictionary identifiers, sense, form and lexeme links,
    with 2.79 million statements in DeltaBot's count.
  - P3865 comes in with 2 statements.
  - P3a's own 13,317 relations include P6307 and P10492, which the WDQS property list of 2026-09-24
    no longer has.
- **Residues** grew slightly: 748 → 751, 765 → 767, 1,441 → 1,453 and 1,446 → 1,458.
- **hw.** Upper bounds fell: 68 → 65 and 60, 61 → 53 and 52. Lower bounds are unchanged (4 and 3).
- **Other bounds moved either way.** ghw upper bounds went 29 → 30 and 31 → 30 (robust) and 39 → 35
  (all). observed-robust's fhw lower bound went 63/19 → 75/23. tw bounds shifted, and the controls'
  tw (the largest relation minus one) grew by 1 or 11. These are different schemas, not a better or
  worse measurement of one.
- **No class changed.**

## Findings, as the note now states them

1. Unchanged: every schema with shared role names is α-cyclic. The slice rows are too.
2. Wikidata's cores have 391 to 1,458 relations, and hw lies in:
   - [4, 38] (declared);
   - [4, 65] and [3, 53] (observed, whole dump);
   - [4, 48] and [4, 51] (observed, slice).

   On the slice the cores and the upper bounds are smaller, but the lower bounds are not.
   Observed-all's is 4 there against 3 over the dump, although each slice relation is a dump
   relation with a subset of its roles. Widths are not monotone under shrinking relations: the
   slice's 53-role clique needs four slice relations to cover, and two dump relations cover it
   (instance of, P31, and has part(s), P527).
3. The comparison with HyperBench holds:
   - declared and observed-robust have hw ≥ 4 and observed-all hw ≥ 3, so all three dump schemas
     are "at least that of every one of HyperBench's 1,113 non-random conjunctive queries, and
     strictly more for two of the three";
   - both observed schemas of the slice have hw ≥ 4.
4. Finding 5 of the note (declared lists overstate use), recomputed from P3a's file over the whole
   dump with the method of research report 02 §2.4:
   - P39 allows 104 qualifiers; 333 occur on its statements, and 36 pass the robust threshold, all
     of them allowed;
   - over the 1,134 properties with an allowed list and main statements, the median Jaccard
     similarity of the allowed and robust sets is 0.30;
   - the same code gives research report 02's SQID figures (328, 34, 1,149, 0.29) exactly.

## The cross-check with P3a

[results/p3a-crosscheck.json](../../projects/p6-schema-width/results/p3a-crosscheck.json) compares
P3a's own `time_model` and `slot` labels, and its rule-8 list, with P6's classification. No time
model differs on any relation, and the rule-8 lists are the same 57 properties. The 5,333 entries
(slice: 4,218) are two vocabulary differences:
- P2241 and P7452, which P3a labels `rank_reason` and P6's schema declares as `meta`: 4,930 entries;
- P1534 on interval relations, which P3a labels `end_cause` and P6 treats as the built-in
  `khg:end_cause`, not a usage: 403 entries.

Neither reaches a measured hypergraph
([IMPLEMENTATION-NOTES §8](../../projects/p6-schema-width/IMPLEMENTATION-NOTES.md)).

## Limits

- The bounds were found under time limits on a shared machine. The load was moderate and is
  recorded per row, but bounds on an idle machine may differ.
- The slice rows describe the schema of P3a's corpus (items with an English Wikipedia article), not
  of Wikidata.
- The re-run has not been reviewed independently; it passed the same test suite as the reviewed
  rows.
