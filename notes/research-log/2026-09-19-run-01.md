# Research run 01 — building the initial knowledge base

## Brief from the repository owner

Questions asked: What is a knowledge hypergraph? How are they constructed? How are they stored, in
which file types? What are the applications? How are they visualised? Expand beyond these questions.
The base must include development history and the current research frontier. Finish with open
questions for further exploration.

## Method

1. Designed the section layout in `CONVENTIONS.md` (ten numbered `kb/` sections, plus `sources/`,
   `projects/`, `schemas/`, `datasets/`, `tools/`).
2. Ran ten parallel research agents, one per section, each doing web research on primary sources
   (papers, specifications, repositories, documentation) and writing cited notes into its section only.
3. Reviewed the output for gaps and contradictions, ran a second pass on gaps.
4. Wrote the cross-cutting documents: top-level `README.md`, `kb/00-index/` (map of content, glossary,
   reading paths, open questions), merged bibliography.
5. Validated with `tools/validate_kb.py`.
