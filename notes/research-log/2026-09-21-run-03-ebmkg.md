# Research run 03 — one paper surfaced by an external article

## Brief

The repository owner asked whether a Medium overview ("Hypergraph RAG: The Third-Generation
Knowledge Retrieval Revolution", Tao An, 12 October 2025) was in the KB, and if not, whether the
two primary sources it cites that the KB lacked add value. The article itself is a secondary
summary of HyperGraphRAG and Hyper-RAG and was not added; its unsourced "100–150 % gains" and
"three generations" framing are not in the primary literature.

## Decisions

- **Added**: Dou, C. et al., *Enhancing LLM Generation with Knowledge Hypergraph for Evidence-Based
  Medicine*, arXiv:2503.16530 (18 Mar 2025, arXiv only, two citations on Semantic Scholar). Reasons:
  earliest two-tier (evidence-under-topic) hypergraph RAG system, predating HyperGraphRAG by nine
  days and cited by none of the later systems; reports Microsoft GraphRAG *below* no retrieval on
  both Qwen2.5-72B and GPT-4o; error analysis that puts retrieval at 6.7 % of failures; a large
  medical hypergraph (806,495 evidence hyperedges) claimed released.
- **Not added**: Peng, B. et al., *Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation: A Survey*, arXiv:2408.08921.
  General GraphRAG survey with no hypergraph content; companion repository frozen since September
  2024; predates every system in the RAG note.

## Verification performed

- Read the arXiv HTML full text of 2503.16530v1: hypergraph definition (§3.1), construction (§3.2),
  IDEP (§4), Table 1 (all six per-task numbers for both models), Table 3, Appendix A.3
  (corpus composition, Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct, 16 A100-80G GPUs for two weeks), A.7 (ablations are of
  IDEP components only), A.9 (error types), Limitations.
- The data link in the paper, `https://drive.google.com/rag4ebm`, returned HTTP 404. No code
  repository is named. Recorded as not obtainable in `datasets/README.md`.
- Cross-citation check: grepped the arXiv full texts of HyperGraphRAG v3, Hyper-RAG (latest HTML)
  and this paper for each other's identifiers and titles. Zero hits in every direction.
- Semantic Scholar: venue arXiv.org, 2 citations. Crossref: no DOI beyond the arXiv one.
- The "about three quarters of errors from coverage" figure appears only in Figure 7, which the
  text extraction does not carry; marked `[unverified]`. The 6.7 % retrieval figure is in the text.
