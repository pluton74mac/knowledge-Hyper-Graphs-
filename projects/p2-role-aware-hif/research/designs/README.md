# Candidate designs (design phase input)

Three independent designs of the P2 contracts, written in parallel on 2026-09-23 from the five
research reports and the director's fixed decisions F1–F15. Each designer built an offline
prototype to check its own claims; the prototype sources and their outputs are kept beside each
design (bytecode and draft fragments removed).

| Design | Angle | Prototype |
|---|---|---|
| [design-A-standards-first.md](design-A-standards-first.md) | Smallest contract a HIF maintainer would accept; C1 as "folded HIF" | [work-A-standards-first/](work-A-standards-first/) |
| [design-B-semantics-first.md](design-B-semantics-first.md) | Unambiguous identity, keys, time, lifecycle; every R01 identity case classified | [work-B-semantics-first/](work-B-semantics-first/) |
| [design-C-consumer-first.md](design-C-consumer-first.md) | Derived from the call sequences of every consumer project | [work-C-consumer-first/](work-C-consumer-first/) |

These are inputs, not the contract. The authoritative design is [../../DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md),
produced by a judge panel, synthesis and adversarial critique over these three.
